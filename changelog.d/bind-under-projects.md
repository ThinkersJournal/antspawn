kind: **FIXED**: closing a bind session under `C:\Projects` failed its read-back
---
`spawn session` bind revoke read each path back with `icacls` and treated any line naming the account as a removal that had not taken. Under `C:\Projects` the account has an inherited deny (`(I)`), so every close of a bind there reported `still names antspawn-run after removal`, kept the journal entry, and could never succeed although the explicit grant and denies were gone (read back with `icacls` on the root and a file). The read-back now counts explicit entries only (`names_explicitly`, unit-tested with an inherited control). Measured on the real account, 2026-11-19: a bind under `C:\Projects` on a throwaway tree with 41 protected paths opened in 27.7 s (about 0.67 s per path: one powershell each) and closed in 3.8 s. The denies are now applied one powershell per 50 paths (`Acl::deny_writes_many`), which took the same open to 1.15 s (close 3.4 s); the grant beat the inherited deny, an edit reached the folder, a protected file stayed unwritable. New test `bind_under_the_projects_folder_beats_the_inherited_deny_and_costs_are_printed` (runner-only). Not measured: a bind on a real project under `C:\Projects`; a tree with hundreds of protected paths.
